⚠️ The Linguistic & Technical Challenge

Why Arabic Spellcheckers Fall Short

Arabic syntax is governed by complex morphological inflection, root systems, and orthographic subtleties that standard English-first proofing tools continually misdiagnose or ignore.

🧩

Hamza & Orthographic Ambiguity

Persistent confusion between Hamzat Al-Wasl and Hamzat Al-Qat' (إلى vs الى, إن vs ان) and improper Ta' Marbuta usage across professional communication.

⚖️

Subject-Verb Gender Agreement

Failure to catch grammatical agreement discrepancies (e.g., المعلمة يساعد vs المعلمة تساعد) and Tanween Al-Fath accusative Alif markers.

🔒

Cloud Data Leakage Risks

Sending confidential legal briefs and corporate correspondence to third-party cloud APIs poses enterprise privacy vulnerabilities.

♿

Accessibility & Assistive Technology Gaps

Lack of native Arabic Text-to-Speech (TTS), voice dictation, and high-contrast reading aids for visually impaired and elderly users.
